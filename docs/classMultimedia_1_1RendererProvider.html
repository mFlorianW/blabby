Detailed Description

Discovers all MediaRenderer devices on the network and handles the connect and disconnect that devices on the network. Every Multimedia::Renderer that was seen is remembered in the Multimedia::RendererStore, is Offline after a restart until it's discovered again and stays known as Offline when it leaves the network. A Multimedia::Renderer whose device doesn't answer anymore goes Offline and is Online again on its next announcement.
