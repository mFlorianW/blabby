Detailed Description

The Shell::FolderArtwork gives a folder, a Container without Artwork and without an album, the Artwork of the first Playable on its first page. A folder holding only further folders, e.g. an artist's folder of albums, gets the Artwork of one of them, chosen at random: one with Artwork of its own when there is one, otherwise the Artwork found in a subfolder the same way, at most three folders deep. A folder is browsed once, when its Artwork is first asked for, and keeps the chosen Artwork.
